# Order alerts — setup and troubleshooting

Plain-language version for vendors. Send this with onboarding, and link it
from the "Sound off" state of the status chip.

---

## Before every shift (10 seconds)

1. Open the dashboard.
2. Look at the top of the screen. It should say **Sound on** in green.
3. Click **Test sound**. You should hear the alert.
4. Walk to where you normally work. Can you still hear it? If not, turn the
   volume up or move the speaker.

If any of those fail, work through the list below.

---

## "Sound off" showing in the header

Click the red **Sound off** chip. That usually fixes it immediately.

If it comes straight back:

- Reload the page (Ctrl+R), then click anywhere on the page.
- Close the browser completely and reopen it.
- Call support.

---

## The alert is silent even though it says "Sound on"

Work down this list in order. The first three are the cause about 90% of the time.

### 1. The tab is muted

Right-click the dashboard tab at the top of the browser. If the menu says
**Unmute site**, click it. Muting a tab silences everything, including alerts.

### 2. Windows has the browser turned down

- Right-click the speaker icon in the bottom-right of the screen.
- Choose **Open Volume Mixer**.
- Find Chrome (or Edge) in the list. Make sure the slider is not at zero
  and the little speaker icon next to it is not crossed out.

### 3. Sound is going to the wrong device

Windows switches to a new speaker automatically whenever one is plugged in.
If someone connected headphones, a Bluetooth speaker, or an HDMI monitor,
the alert may be playing into that instead.

- Click the speaker icon in the bottom-right.
- Click the arrow next to the volume slider.
- Pick the speaker you can actually hear.

To test: play any YouTube video. If that is silent too, the problem is the
computer's sound, not the dashboard.

### 4. The site is blocked from playing sound

- Click the icon just left of the web address at the top.
- Choose **Site settings**.
- Find **Sound** and set it to **Allow**.
- Reload the page.

### 5. Nothing above worked

Call support and tell them:

- Which browser you use (Chrome or Edge)
- Whether the header says Sound on or Sound off
- Whether you can hear a YouTube video
- Whether the Test sound button does anything at all

---

## The alert stops after a few seconds

It should not. The alert is meant to keep ringing until you press **Accept**.
If it stops on its own, report it — that is a bug on our side, not a setting
on yours.

---

## The alert never comes at all

Check the **Connected** indicator next to the sound chip.

- If it says **Reconnecting**, your internet has dropped. The dashboard will
  catch up automatically once it comes back, and any orders that arrived
  while you were offline will ring then.
- If it says **Connected** and an order still did not ring, report it with
  the order number. We can see exactly what happened on our side.

---

## Recommended setup

- **A separate USB speaker** on the wall or shelf where you work, rather than
  relying on the speakers built into the monitor. Around $15 and it is the
  single biggest improvement you can make.
- **Leave the dashboard open** in its own window, not as one tab among twenty.
- **Do not let the computer sleep.** Support can set this for you.
- **Keep your mobile number up to date.** If an order is not accepted within
  a minute we will text you, and then call. That only works if we have the
  right number.
